# Demo script (about 3 minutes)

Use a laptop or tablet, landscape, sound on. Start from a fresh browser profile, or use **Backup → Clear all data** first.

## 0:00 – Set up (20 s)

1. Open the app. The child home says “Ask a grown-up to add a learner.”
2. Press and hold the small lock in the bottom-right corner for 2 seconds (a quick tap does nothing). *“The lock keeps children out; it isn’t security. Everything stays on this device.”*
3. **Backup → Load demo data.** Two demo learners appear: Asha and Kabir.

## 0:20 – The position-bias story (50 s)

4. **Progress**, learner **Kabir**, lesson **Can**.
5. Point at the trend: Kabir sits at about 50 % in Check for a week. *“On paper it looks like he half-knows it.”*
6. Scroll to **Position bias**: 85 % of his taps are on the right, and the panel is flagged. *“He isn’t answering; he’s choosing the right-hand card. The sides are counterbalanced (5 left, 5 right, never more than 2 in a row), so a side preference shows up as exactly this 50 %.”*
7. *“A therapist would now look at his seating or the tablet position, or try errorless teaching with him.”* Open **Learners** and show Kabir’s settings: strategy, token board of 3, mastery criterion.

## 1:10 – Plain-language summary and report (25 s)

8. **Progress → Asha → Can’t.** Read the summary: *“Answers the lion questions independently; still needs help with who can’t roar.”*
9. **Print or save as PDF**: the print preview shows only the report with the centre’s name and the date.

## 1:35 – A lesson, as the child sees it (70 s)

10. **Back to child view → Change learner → Asha.** Can is mastered (and due for review), Can’t is in progress, and the sticker book has her Can sticker.
11. Open **Can’t**. Pick a reward (**Bubbles**). Note the schedule strip (Watch → Practise → Check → Reward) and the “First… Then…” token board.
12. **Watch**: one slide. The guide reads “The baby can’t swim. The swimmer can swim.” while the read-along highlights each word, and a hand-drawn circle marks the one who can’t. Tap **Next** through the ten slides (one per item; each one can be replayed); the last one says **Practise**.
13. **Practise**: “Look.” then the question, e.g. “Who can’t fly?”. Wait: the correct card glows, grows, and the guide points (“This one.”). Tap it: praise, and a token flies to the board.
14. Tap the **wrong** card on the next trial. *“No red, no buzz, no shake. The guide says ‘Let’s try again’, shows the full prompt, and it’s recorded as prompted, not independent.”*
15. Tap **Break**: a calm screen with no timer. Tap **Ready** and the same trial comes back.

## 2:45 – Close (15 s)

16. Back in the grown-up area, **Lessons**: items are editable, draft rows never reach a child, a Google Sheet CSV can be pasted in with per-row validation, and **Reset to DCCD deck** restores the original 10.
17. *“Every teaching number (time delays, mastery, review after 7 days) is a default in one file for DCCD’s clinicians to review.”*
