# Demo script (about 3 minutes)

Use a laptop or tablet, landscape, sound on. Start from a fresh browser profile, or use **Backup → Clear all data** first.

## 0:00 – Set up (20 s)

1. Open the app. The first page says “Who is reading today?” and “Ask a grown-up to add a reader.”
2. Tap **Grown-ups** in the bottom-right corner. *“Everything stays on this device.”*
3. **Backup → Load demo data.** Two demo learners appear: Asha and Kabir.

## 0:20 – The position-bias story (50 s)

4. **Progress**, learner **Kabir**, lesson **Can**.
5. Point at the trend: Kabir sits at about 50 % in Check for a week. *“On paper it looks like he half-knows it.”*
6. Scroll to **Position bias**: 85 % of his taps are on the right, and the panel is flagged. *“He isn’t answering; he’s choosing the right-hand card. The sides are counterbalanced (5 left, 5 right, never more than 2 in a row), so a side preference shows up as exactly this 50 %.”*
7. *“A therapist would now look at his seating or the tablet position.”* Open **Children**, open Kabir’s **More settings**: voice speed, calm mode, books on his shelf, mastery criterion.

## 1:10 – Plain-language summary and report (25 s)

8. **Progress → Asha → Can’t.** Read the summary: *“Answers the lion questions independently; still needs help with who can’t roar.”*
9. **Print or save as PDF**: the print preview shows only the report with the centre’s name and the date.

## 1:35 – A lesson, as the child sees it (70 s)

10. **Back to child view → Change reader → Asha.** Her bookshelf: **Who can?** says *Read again* (mastered, due for review), **Who can’t?** says *Keep going*, and her sticker page has the Can sticker.
11. Open **Who can’t?**. The cover; tap **Open the book**. *“Only three buttons: home, voice speed, sound.”*
12. A page: the guide reads “Who can’t fly?” in a slow, calm voice while each word lights up. *“Nothing is marked yet: no circle, no glow, no hint.”*
13. Tap the right picture: the hand-drawn circle draws itself around it, a “can’t” tag appears, and the guide says “Yes! The snake can’t fly.” Then the corner arrow appears; tap it and the page turns.
14. On the next page tap the **wrong** picture. *“No red, no buzz, no shake. ‘Let’s look again.’”* Tap wrong again: now the circle shows the answer and the guide explains it. It’s recorded as a correction, not independent.
15. Tap the snail/rabbit button: the line is read again, slower or faster. The choice is saved for this child.

## 2:45 – Close (15 s)

16. Back in the grown-up area, **Books → New page**: type “hop”, tap the bird and the snake, tap who can’t; the preview is the child’s page. Hidden pages never reach a child; **Import from a sheet** takes a Google Sheet CSV with per-row validation, and **Reset to DCCD deck** restores the original 10.
17. *“Every teaching number (mastery, review after 7 days) is a default in one file for DCCD’s clinicians to review.”*
